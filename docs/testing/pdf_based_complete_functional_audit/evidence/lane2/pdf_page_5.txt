    sakta hai.

 · Family Chat / SOS / Focus Mode: AirDroid feature pages/overview mein family communication, child-triggered SOS

9. Background access: AirDroid Kids ko background mein
kaise active rakha jata hai

Parental-control features tabhi reliable rehte hain jab child-side service background mein chal sake. Android battery
management aur phone manufacturer ke task managers apps ko suspend/kill kar sakte hain. AirDroid khud kehta hai
ki Android background process ko permanently alive rakhne ki guarantee developer nahi de sakta.

Common background/keep-alive settings

 · Autostart / Auto-launch: phone restart ke baad AirDroid Kids ko launch hone ki permission.
 · Run in background / background activity: OEM app manager mein app ko background restriction se exempt karna.
 · Battery optimization: Unrestricted / Don't optimize: battery saver se app ki background activity aggressively stop

    na ho.
 · Power saving mode: aggressive battery saver ko disable ya app-specific exception dena.
 · Unrestricted background data: mobile data/Wi-Fi background network access allow karna; data saver restrictions

    check karna.
 · Wi-Fi always on during sleep: kuch devices mein sleep ke waqt Wi-Fi disconnect na ho.
 · Recent apps lock / cleanup allowlist: kuch OEMs mein app ko cleaner/task killer se protect karna.
 · AirDroid Kids service & permissions: app updated rahe aur required permissions revoke na hon.
Settings ke exact names Samsung, Xiaomi/Redmi, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, OnePlus, Motorola aur Android version ke
hisaab se badalte hain. AirDroid ki official help guide device-specific keep-alive instructions follow karne ko kehti hai.
