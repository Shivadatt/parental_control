### 2.5 Cross-cutting observations

- Usage data is genuinely collected and synced, not fabricated: `UsageReporter.kt:95-104` states “Real UsageStatsManager data only; never fabricates values”, and the live run uploaded 32 real rows (`docs/verification-report.md:579`). The gap is presentation and enforcement, not collection.
- The dashboard is a **device-status** dashboard, not a **Today's Event** dashboard: `dashboard_controller.dart:96-134` loads family, members, devices and the latest policy only; there is no usage, alert-count, trend or event query. AirDroid's “Today” is therefore not modelled.
- Alert generation is far narrower than the UI implies: two insert sites (`device-telemetry/index.ts:129-147,300-351`) versus six enum types (`lib/data/models/telemetry.dart:203-249`) and copy promising offline/permission alerts (`alerts_body.dart:81-82`).
- Screen-time policy is written and acknowledged but not enforced on the child: `PolicyApplier.kt:297` comment; `dailyScreenTimeLimitMinutes` unused outside parsing. This is the largest functional misrepresentation in this area.
- Presence has three independent, documented defects (BUG-038 false Offline, BUG-039 stale sync after relink, BUG-041 inconsistent last-seen) plus F2 (stalled poll), so “online”, “last seen” and “device health” must be read as best-effort.
- Server-authoritative behaviour is consistently good: unlink waits for server confirmation (`family_controller.dart:160-165`), revocation is server-side, alert acknowledgement is server-first (`safety_alerts_controller.dart:100-119`), one active device per child is a DB constraint (`core.sql:117-119`).

## 4. One-paragraph summary a reviewer can trust

Family and device management is the strongest part of the app: authentication and server-assigned roles, Child-ID/QR pairing with mandatory child approval, device registration with a per-child single-active-device constraint, server-confirmed unlinking and revocation, account switching and session restore are all implemented end to end and backed by live evidence (`docs/verification-report.md:570-597`). The dashboard, by contrast, is only a device-status card with 60-second polling: daily/weekly app usage is genuinely collected by `UsageStatsManager`, uploaded hourly and stored in `usage_daily`, but it is displayed only on a device-scoped Screen-time screen, and notification counts, cellular data, trends, activity feeds, historical summaries and dashboard alert badges do not exist. Screen-time limits and bedtime are the most damaging gap: the parent saves them, the child device parses and echoes them, and nothing enforces them (`PolicyApplier.kt:297`), so the product promises control it does not deliver. Alerts are thin: only low-battery and geofence enter/exit are generated server-side, while device-offline, permission, app-update, security, social and image alerts are absent even though the empty state advertises offline and permission alerts, and there is no push channel at all (FCM not configured). Presence and last-seen carry open P2 defects (false Offline for 2 of every 5 minutes, a 42-minute poll stall, revoked devices listed as current), and remote commands are limited to a policy sync because the other four Kotlin handlers have no UI. Everything claimed above is either cited code, a named automated test suite, or a specific runtime document; the unverified items are collected in section 3.
